Ember

An evening companion that keeps a goal alive by carving it into three bands — Stretch, Realistic, Floor — refit from real evidence, never from mood.

status: approved Web app · local Proof of concept First user: you
01

The unique kernel

Ember refuses to let a goal shrink below the version that still counts.

Every goal is a band, not a number, and the floor can never be argued to zero — not by a tired evening, not by “I feel unstoppable, raise everything.”

Bands move only on evidence from logs, never on mood alone. A small win is a real win.

05

Inspiration & identity

Solo Leveling’s “System”

An interface that appears, sets the bar, and refuses to let you scale down to comfort — softened to be humane. Never life-threatening; always a floor that counts.

en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling

Shonen energy

Persistence, showing up when you don’t feel like it, remembering your why and what’s at stake. Favorites: Bleach, Naruto, Demon Slayer, Solo Leveling, Attack on Titan, Black Clover.

The feel

Calm, warm, evening-toned — an ember glow, not a productivity dashboard. The learner works in hyper-real pencil, so the aesthetic should carry craft and stillness.

06

Why this matters

“I skipped the gym in my head at 6:15pm because it was ‘too late,’ then a random reminder of why I was doing it got me there for a 30-minute session, and I was glad I didn’t miss the day.” Ember does that on purpose, every night. Started for one person; turned out to be needed by many.

The boundary

What’s in now, what waits, what’s out — each with a reason.

  • Goal interview (8–10 questions) — routine, activities, plans, goals, honest capacity.
  • Band calibration — intake → Stretch/Realistic/Floor with reasons; floor-protection refusal; one-tap nightly log → evidence-based reforge.
  • Single goal — one goal at a time, carrying its own three bands.
  • Evening shutdown — brain dump → top 3 with a tiny first step → “done for today.”
  • Reminders — in-app pop-up in your own words + one-tap floor start.
  • Weekly check-in — reads accumulated logs and proposes band adjustments.
  • OS-level / scheduled notifications — real push so reminders fire when the app is closed.
  • Passive day-sensing & integrations — calendar/health data to enrich evidence automatically.
  • Multi-goal — managing several goals at once, each with its own bands.
  • Deeper history & analytics — streaks and band drift over longer horizons.
  • Accounts / multi-user / sign-in — single-person is enough to prove the loop.
  • Sync / cloud persistence — local storage proves the loop.
  • Native mobile app — a local web app demonstrates the whole idea.
  • Passive tracking / surveillance-style sensing — contradicts the “you tell it, it learns” design.
