# Ember

An evening companion that keeps a goal alive by carving it into three bands — **Stretch, Realistic, Floor** — refit from real evidence, never from mood.

## The Unique Kernel
Ember refuses to let a goal shrink below the version that still counts. Every goal is a **band**, not a number, and the **floor can never be argued to zero** — not by a tired evening, not by "I feel unstoppable, raise everything." Bands move only on **evidence from logs**, never on mood alone. A small win is a real win.

## Who It's For
**You first**, and then busy adults like you: juggling deadlines and personal goals (fitness, study, side projects), who start strong, stall in the evening, and want a gentle way to end the day and keep the smallest version alive. Today they use habit trackers that trust a single target and therefore swing with mood, and quit when the target stops fitting the day.

## The Core Loop
1. **Open Ember** — it asks, "What do you want to keep alive?"
2. **Name a goal** (e.g. "Gym"). A first-time **detailed interview (8–10 questions)** builds a picture of the person's life: routine, activities, plans, goals, honest capacity.
3. **See the bands** — Stretch / Realistic / Floor, each with a one-line reason drawn from the answers.
4. **Try to cheat it** — zero the floor or "raise everything." Ember **holds the floor**, nudges only the Stretch, and says why.
5. **Evening shutdown** — a messy brain dump becomes **tomorrow's top 3**, each with a tiny first step, then a **"done for today"** screen.
6. **Nightly log** — one tap: Stretch / Realistic / Floor / Nothing.
7. **Reforge** — accumulated evidence re-carves the bands.
8. **Weekly check-in** — reads the logs and proposes band adjustments.
9. **Reminders** — in the learner's own words, ending "Do the floor and it still counts," with a one-tap start on the smallest version.

They come back because Ember keeps the goal *honest and alive* instead of letting a hard day erase it.

## Inspiration & Identity
- **Solo Leveling's "System"** — an interface that appears, sets the bar, and refuses to let you scale down to comfort. Ember is that mechanic, deliberately **softened to be humane** (never life-threatening, always a floor that counts).
  - Reference: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solo_Leveling
- **Shonen energy** — persistence, showing up when you don't feel like it, remembering your *why* and what's at stake. Favorites: *Bleach*, *Naruto*, *Demon Slayer*, *Solo Leveling*, *Attack on Titan*, *Black Clover*.
- The learner is a **hyper-realism pencil artist**; the feel should be calm, warm, evening-toned — an ember glow, not a productivity dashboard.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
They plan big when motivated and quit when tired; they're used to immediate gratification and struggle to stick with things long. The founding moment: "I skipped the gym in my head at 6:15pm because it was 'too late,' then a random reminder of why I was doing it got me there for a 30-minute session, and I was glad I didn't miss the day." Ember does that on purpose, every night. It started as a tool for one person and turned out to be needed by many.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A ~1-minute demo, local web app:
- Opens to a single empty screen: **"What do you want to keep alive?"**
- Name one goal; answer the interview; see **three bands with reasons** drawn from the answers.
- Dump a messy head into the **evening shutdown**; get **top 3 with tiny first steps** and a "done for today" screen.
- **One-tap nightly log.**
- **The refusal beat:** try to zero the floor or "raise everything" — Ember keeps the floor, nudges only the Stretch, and explains why. *(The "oh, that's Ember" moment.)*
- Activate reminders: an in-app pop-up in the learner's own words ends **"Do the floor and it still counts,"** with a one-tap start on the smallest version.
- **Single goal:** one goal at a time, shown with its own three bands. (Multi-goal is deferred — see Later.)

## The POC Boundary
In, at full depth:
- **Goal interview:** 8–10 questions covering routine, activities, plans, goals, honest capacity.
- **Band calibration:** intake → Stretch/Realistic/Floor with reasons; **floor-protection refusal**; one-tap nightly log → **evidence-based reforge**.
- **Single goal:** one goal at a time, with its own three bands.
- **Evening shutdown:** brain dump → top 3 with a tiny first step → "done for today."
- **Reminders:** in-app pop-up in the learner's own words + one-tap floor start.
- **Weekly check-in:** reads accumulated logs and proposes band adjustments.

## Later
- **OS-level / scheduled notifications** (real push, not in-app), so reminders fire when the app is closed.
- **Passive day-sensing and integrations** (calendar, health) to enrich evidence automatically.
- **Multi-goal:** managing several goals at once, each with its own bands.
- Longer history and richer analytics on streaks and band drift.

## Explicitly Cut
- **Accounts / multi-user / sign-in** — Ember is single-person for the proof of concept.
- **Sync / cloud persistence** — local storage is enough to prove the loop.
- **Native mobile app** — a local web app demonstrates the whole idea.
- **Passive tracking / surveillance-style sensing** — contradicts Ember's "you tell it, it learns" design and isn't needed to prove the kernel.
