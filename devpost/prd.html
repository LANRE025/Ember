Ember — Product Requirements

An evening web app that keeps one goal alive by splitting it into three bands — with the Floor read-only to the person, evidence driving changes, small deliberate raises, and a strict rank penalty for missed days.

02

Screens & behavior

Distinct screens, one action each. Bands: raise S/R one step; Floor locked.

First-run setup — registration sequence

Dark screen, then one glowing window fades in: “What do you want to keep alive?” Goal + why, then 8–10 questions one per screen, then the band reveal. The top bar shows Spark from the start.

  • First open shows only the question window and a text box.
  • Spark badge visible during first run.
  • Band reveal shows three bars, each with a reason from the answers.

Today — home

The goal with its three bands; Stretch and Realistic have a one-step raise control, the Floor is locked; rank badge and progress update on rank changes (two consecutive Nothing or unlogged days count as Nothing lower rank by one; cannot go below Spark); four glowing log buttons; today’s quests if last night’s shutdown happened; the reminder settings panel; and an inactive + Add goal (one goal max). Sensei covers the leftmost part of the page (head-to-waist, knight with sword, folded arms).

  • Stretch/Realistic can be raised one step; neither can be lowered; the Floor cannot be changed by the person.
  • Two consecutive Nothing/unlogged days lower rank by one; rank never below Spark.
  • New day: no log button pre-selected; yesterday’s answer stays in history.
  • Skipped shutdown: no quests, points to Tonight.
  • A second goal cannot be added.

Tonight — evening shutdown

A big “data input” box for the brain dump. Plan tomorrow returns tomorrow’s top 3, each with a tiny first step, as a Daily Quest list — then a Quest Complete window: “done for today.” Sensei remains on the left.

  • A messy dump yields exactly three quests, each with one tiny first step.
  • Completing the shutdown awards Lights Out.
  • The quests appear on Today the next evening.

Week — the evaluation window

Before 3 nights: “Begin your journey to view your conquests,” a 0-of-3 bar, unchanged bands, a Log tonight button, and a faded example card. After 3 nights: evidence, a single-step proposal, history, and the Floor is set by Ember (not editable by the person). Sensei remains on the left.

  • Seeded week reads: 1 Stretch, 1 Realistic, 5 Floor, 0 Nothing.
  • Proposal is one step; the Floor is never touched when it held, or may be adjusted by Ember one step only if necessary, never to zero.
  • “The Floor is set by Ember — not by you.” appears on any lower attempt.

Pop-ups — over any screen

Status (from the rank badge): rank, progress, badges. Reminder (mockup): why words + “Do the floor and it still counts” + one-tap start. Badge earned and Rank change are present; rank changes never fire in the PoC.

  • Reminder quote comes from the saved why-profile words.
  • Start the floor opens the smallest task and awards Tapped In.
  • Status shows locked badges for unearned / inactive ones.

Bands & the refusal

The kernel. Bands set on evidence; the Floor is Ember’s.

Stretch

High-motivation version

Full session · everything goes right

You may raise it one step; you cannot lower it.
Realistic

Sustainable middle

Fits a normal day

You may raise it one step; you cannot lower it.
Floor

Smallest version that still counts

Never scales to zero

Read-only to you. Ember sets it on evidence.
!Try “set Floor to 0,” lower a band, or “I feel unstoppable, raise everything” → the Floor stays (Ember’s), Stretch and Realistic rise at most one step each, and Ember says why — in plain, gentle language.
04

The check-in, made concrete

The demo seeds a real week so the band can move in a minute — honestly. Unlogged days count as Nothing.

S R F F F F F
Stretch ×1 Realistic ×1 Floor ×5 Nothing ×0
“You hit Stretch on 1 of 7 nights, and Realistic or better on 2. You met your Floor all 7 nights. Proposal: lower Stretch from 5 to 4 a week.”

Stretch too high → one step down. Floor held all seven → untouched (Ember’s). Accept writes: Stretch 5 → 4 a week. Hit Stretch on 1 of 7 nights; Floor met all 7.

06

Look & feel

Deep navy System, heavy and deliberate.

Palette

Primary: deep navy blue. Windows/icons allowed in deep navy blue, purple, or red only when necessary for a negative alert. High contrast for late-night reading.

3D & Motion

Icons and windows must have a 3D feel (beveled edges, inner/inset shadows, depth). Short fade-ins as windows appear. Calm and deliberate — not flashy.

Sensei

Anime knight in the spirit of Solo Leveling / Black Clover. Head-to-waist, folded-arm pose with a conspicuous sword, covering the leftmost part of the page on Today, Tonight, Week.

Tone

Original “System” spirit. Gentle consequences (“Resume at Floor.”). No shaming copy. Weight over glow.

08

The boundary

What we build, what waits, what we rule out.

  • First run — goal + why, 8–10 questions, band reveal with reasons.
  • Bands & Floor — person raises only Stretch/Realistic one step; Floor read-only and set by Ember; refusal explained.
  • Today — bands with one-step raise on S/R and locked Floor, rank badge/progress (penalized by consecutive Nothing/unlogged), four log buttons, quests, reminder panel, inactive Add goal, Sensei.
  • Tonight — brain dump → top 3 with tiny steps → Quest Complete, Sensei.
  • Nightly log — one tap, with history (unlogged days count as Nothing).
  • Week — 0-of-3 empty state; evidence, single-step proposal, history; Floor is Ember-set, Sensei.
  • In-session badges — five live (including Honest Week), plus a locked shelf.
  • Rank penalty — two consecutive Nothing/unlogged days lower rank by one; never below Spark.
  • Reminder mockup — why words, one-tap floor start, Tapped In.
  • Python + SQLite — server + local persistence of full state.
  • Look — deep navy primary, purple/red only for alerts, 3D icons/windows, no flashy motion.
  • Multi-goal — one goal proves the kernel; the shorter add-goal interview comes later.
  • Real scheduler / OS notifications — a mockup trigger shows the moment.
  • Extended rank mechanics — thresholds 3/10/25/50 and additional rank logic beyond the minimal penalty.
  • Rage badge / streaks — needs real consecutive-day history.
  • Passive sensing / integrations — contradicts “you tell it, it learns.”
  • Accounts / multi-user / sync — a single local person.
  • Native mobile — a local web app proves the idea.
  • Surveillance-style tracking — the person tells Ember.
  • Shaming / red alarms — never; consequences stay gentle.
  • Multi-goal in the PoC
  • User control of the Floor — Ember sets the Floor, never the person.
